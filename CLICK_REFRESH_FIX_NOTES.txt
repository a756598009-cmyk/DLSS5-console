Click refresh fix:
- Root cause: ToggleAt() unconditionally called SaveSettings() and Invalidate(FALSE),
  even when the click was on blank space or non-interactive text.
- ToggleAt() now tracks whether a real toggle state changed. It saves and invalidates
  only after a valid toggle click.
- Blank/decorative clicks no longer write the INI file or trigger a full repaint.
- Removed the default CDialog::OnLButtonDown call from the custom-painted surface;
  custom hit-testing remains responsible for its controls.
- Dropdown, tab, slider, scroll, and admin-toggle event handling is otherwise preserved.
Build:
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\BUILD.ps1
