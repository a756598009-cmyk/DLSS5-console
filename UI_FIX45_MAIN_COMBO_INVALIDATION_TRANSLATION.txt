Fix45 - Main-page dropdown popup invalidation coordinate fix

Issue:
The FSR 4 dropdown opened briefly and disappeared. Main-page content and popup
are drawn under a -140px Y translation, but InvalidateComboRegion invalidated
its pre-translation anchor/popup rectangle. Windows therefore clipped the paint
update to an area 140px too low, leaving the actual popup outside the update
region.

Fix:
For main-page combo popups (excluding the fixed target combo), offset the
invalidation rectangle by -140px before calling InvalidateDesignRect. Settings
combos remain fixed and are not translated.

No visual layout, option ordering, or selection behavior changed.
