Fix12 - Larger UI, resizable window, minimize/maximize

Changes:
- Increased initial client window size from 620x1280 to a screen-aware 760x1450 (bounded by screen dimensions).
- Enabled WS_THICKFRAME, WS_SYSMENU, WS_MINIMIZEBOX and WS_MAXIMIZEBOX while retaining the custom popup-style title bar.
- Added custom minimize, maximize/restore and close buttons in the title bar.
- Added a minimum tracking size of 620x900.
- Kept the existing 600x1240 design coordinate system and its DrawInterface scaling, so drawing and hit-testing continue to map through ToDesignPoint during resizing.
- Increased shared GDI+ text scale from 1.30 to 1.42, including combo and HUD preview measurement fonts.
- Preserved all three tabs, settings, NRLive integration, profile/configuration behavior and shortcut handling.

Validation:
