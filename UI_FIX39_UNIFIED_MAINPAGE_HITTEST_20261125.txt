Fix39 - Unified main-page hit-test coordinate space
Root cause:
- The main-page tabs and feature content are drawn with a -140px Y translation.
- Different controls were hit-tested in mixed coordinate spaces. Some paths
  added the translation, others did not; drag and wheel paths also omitted it.
Fix:
- Normalize main-page input coordinates once in OnLButtonDown, OnMouseMove,
  and OnMouseWheel before testing content hit rectangles.
- Keep fixed header/settings controls in their unshifted coordinate space.
- Remove the second tab-only translation in HitTest to avoid double shifting.
- This aligns combo boxes, sliders, toggles, NR controls/groups, overlay tiles,
  expansion buttons, tabs, scrollbar and wheel handling with the rendered page.
- No visual layout changes.
- VS2022 build and runtime validation are still required.
