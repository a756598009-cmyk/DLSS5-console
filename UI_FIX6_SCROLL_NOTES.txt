UI Fix6 - content overflow and wheel repaint

1. Reduced the scrollable page drawing clip from x=25..573 to x=25..558,
   reserving the right gutter for the fixed scrollbar so page controls/text
   cannot paint beneath or beyond the scrollbar lane.
2. Reworked OnMouseWheel:
   - If a custom combo popup or its anchor is under the pointer, consume the
     wheel without scrolling the page or causing a repaint. Combo lists are
     short and fully visible, so no internal list scrolling is needed.
   - Page scrolling invalidates only the scrollable page rectangle. The
     header, capture settings, tabs and footer are not invalidated.
   - No Invalidate(FALSE) full-window refresh is issued for wheel scrolling.

No controls, tabs, settings, or runtime features were removed.
Windows/MSVC build and live UI behavior still require user-side verification.
