Fix14 - Remove white margins around the resizable UI

Cause:
DrawInterface uses uniform scaling and centers the 600x1240 design canvas.
When the outer window has a different aspect ratio, unused client-area margins
remain outside the canvas. The previous OnEraseBkgnd returned TRUE without
painting, and OnPaint did not clear the full client surface, allowing the
MFC dialog's default white background to show through.

Changes:
- OnEraseBkgnd fills the entire client rectangle with RGB(17,15,23), matching
  the dark UI background.
- OnPaint clears the entire GDI+ client surface with the same color before
  drawing the centered interface.
- The uniform scaling, aspect ratio, resize behavior, title buttons, and all
  UI/control logic remain unchanged.

Validation:
- Source-level patch applied.
- ZIP integrity checked after packaging.
- Not compiled or run in Windows/VS2022 in this environment.
