Fix41 - Scroll-aware control hit-testing correction

Audit findings and changes:
- NR group/control hit rectangles and NR slider rectangles are stored in
  original content coordinates. Their click and drag paths now always restore
  m_scrollOffset after the page's -140 draw translation.
- Standard combo boxes and standard sliders already store hit rectangles with
  m_scrollOffset applied; their paths intentionally do NOT add it again.
- Generic ToggleAt/HitTest regions use original content coordinates. The
  remaining main-page click path now restores m_scrollOffset unconditionally.
- Wheel scrolling is explicitly limited to the main-page scrollable content;
  fixed header/tabs and the Settings page are excluded.
- No visual layout changes.

Important validation:
- This is a source-level coordinate correction. It still requires VS2022
  compilation and interactive runtime testing, especially after scrolling.
