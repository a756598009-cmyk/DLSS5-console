Fix50 - NR Choice popup mouse-wheel ownership

- When an NR Choice popup is open on the Neural Rendering tab, wheel input over
  the popup or its anchor is consumed by the popup interaction.
- The wheel no longer scrolls the underlying NR page while the pointer is over
  the open popup/anchor.
- Pointer coordinates are converted to the same scrolled content coordinate
  space used by the NR popup and hit rectangles.
- Wheel input outside the popup and anchor continues to scroll the main page.
- No NR values, option order, config keys, or UI layout were changed.

Static source change only. Build and runtime behavior must be verified in VS2022.
