Fix42 - Additional interaction audit fixes

Two concrete issues found in Fix41:
1. Settings-only hit rectangles (capture target, hotkeys, Bind Bypass) were
   tested even when Settings was hidden. Their stale rectangles could overlap
   the main feature pages and intercept clicks. They are now tested only while
   m_settingsOpen is true.
2. Standard slider hit rectangles were not cleared before drawing a new page.
   A slider from a previously visited tab could remain clickable while hidden.
   The slider rectangle array is now cleared at the beginning of DrawInterface.

No visual layout changes. VS2022 compilation and runtime tests are still needed.
