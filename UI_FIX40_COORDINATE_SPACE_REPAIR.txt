Fix40 - Main page coordinate handling correction

Corrections based on a source audit of Fix39:
1. Main-page tabs are visible at design Y=340..383 after the -140px draw
   translation. Click normalization now begins at Y=340, not Y=375.
2. Tabs are hit-tested immediately after the fixed -140px translation and
   BEFORE page scroll offset is added. Tabs therefore remain fixed while
   feature content scrolls.
3. Generic HitTest no longer handles tabs after content scroll normalization.
4. During mouse capture/drag, OnMouseMove applies the same -140px page
   coordinate conversion continuously, including when the pointer moves
   outside the visible content viewport.
5. Scroll-wheel conversion remains limited to the scrollable content region.

Existing per-control coordinate conventions are preserved:
- combo/standard slider hit rectangles already include -scrollOffset;
- NR/group hit rectangles are converted by adding scrollOffset at click time;
- fixed tabs are not affected by scrollOffset;
- fixed header and Settings controls are tested before main-page conversion.

No visual layout changes. VS2022 build and runtime testing are still required.
